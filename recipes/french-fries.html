French Fries Recipe:

Click here to navigate Back to Index Page

The above is a image of the dish French-Fries.

It's a very popular dish made from boiled potatoes

It is best served hot with mayo and tomato ketchup

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Cooking oil (vegetable or sunflower)
  • Salt
  • Cold water

Steps

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into thin, even strips.
  2. Rinse the strips in cold water to remove excess starch, then soak them for 20 - 30 minutes.
  3. Drain and pat the potatoes completely dry using a clean cloth or paper towel.
  4. Heat oil in a deep pan on medium heat and fry the potatoes until they are soft but not browned (about 3 - 4 minutes). Remove and let them rest for a few minutes.
  5. Increase the oil heat and fry the potatoes again until golden and crispy.
  6. Remove from oil, drain excess oil, sprinkle salt, and serve hot.
