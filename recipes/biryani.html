Biryani Recipe:

Click here to navigate Back to Index Page

The above is a image of the dish Biryani.

The main ingredient here is steamed rice, taken of 2-3 varieties for making it more attractive.

It is best served hot with gravy and riata.

Ingredients

  • Steamed Rice
  • Cloves, Garlic, Cardamom and black pepper
  • Oil, Sesame seeds
  • Vegetables (4 - 5 varieties)
  • Salt, Red Chilli powder, Coriander powder, Biryani mix spice powder
  • Coriander and fried onions

Steps

  1. Cook/Steam the rice and keep it aside
  2. Add oil and all the spices in a pan
  3. Boil all the veggies and drain
  4. Mix the veggies with all the powders and salt.
  5. Start layering thing in the pan.
  6. Let oil+spices stay at the bottom start adding veggies and rice alternatively in the pan
  7. at last top of the rice+veggie layers with coriander and fried onion and cook for 15 mins with the lid on the pan.
  8. The dish is ready to eat, just garnish it with more coriander,lemon slices and fried onion.
