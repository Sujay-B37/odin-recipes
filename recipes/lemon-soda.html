Lemon Soda Recipe:

Click here to navigate Back to Index Page

The above is a image of Lemon-Soda.

It's a cool and refreshing drink made of carbonated water.

It is made fresh and served chilled.

Ingredients

  • Lemon
  • Sugar
  • Ice cubes
  • Cold water

Steps

  1. Wash the lemon and squeeze out the fresh juice into a glass.
  2. Add sugar to the lemon juice and stir until it dissolves completely.
  3. Add cold water to the glass and mix well.
  4. Drop in a few ice cubes.
  5. Taste and adjust sugar or lemon juice if needed.
  6. Stir once more and serve chilled.
